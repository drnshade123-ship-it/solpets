SOL PETS - EXTERNAL LEVEL SOUNDS

All tracks are original 12-second mono WAV loops.
Format: 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM, mono.

01-meadow.wav - Warm meadow plucks and light birdsong
02-snow-course.wav - Airy snow wind and distant glass bells
03-cave-course.wav - Low cavern drone with echoing mineral drops
04-night-meadow.wav - Heavy rain, moonlit pads, and soft bells
05-ice-cave.wav - Crystalline arpeggios and cold cave wind
06-morning-meadow.wav - Bright sunrise mallets and cheerful chimes
sol-pets-pond-meadow.wav - Gentle watery bells, reeds, bubbles, and lily-pad percussion
07-lava-volcano.wav - Deep volcanic pulse, rumble, and stone drums
08-rainbow-run.wav - Fast colorful synth arpeggio and sparkles

POND MEADOW HTML MAPPING

The Pond Meadow track is connected in index.html as:

pond: "level-sounds/sol-pets-pond-meadow.wav"

The existing biome music system loops the file automatically, follows the
Music On/Off setting and separate Music Volume slider, and changes to
07-lava-volcano.wav when Lava begins. Music defaults to 30% and the slider is
capped at 30%; SFX has its own independent 0-100% slider.
